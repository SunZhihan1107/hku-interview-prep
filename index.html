🔥 追问准备：BERTopic 原理 + 连接语用推理
What is BERTopic? I roughly read a paper my advisor shared with me — it was an engineering PhD student's thesis. Basically, it converts documents into vectors (like numerical representations), and then extracts topics from these vectors by clustering similar documents together.

Connection to pragmatic inference: In my discourse analysis paper, I used BERTopic to identify recurring discourse patterns in job-seeking narratives. These patterns reveal how speakers implicitly convey meanings beyond literal content — for example, using metaphors like "挂了" (hung up/failed) or "上岸" (made it ashore/succeeded) to construct identity and negotiate face. This is essentially pragmatic inference at scale: computational methods help us discover systematic patterns in how people say things they don't literally mean.
BERTopic 是什么？我大致看了一篇我导师分享给我的论文，是一篇工科博士的毕业论文。大概就是把documents 变成向量（就是数值化的表示），然后再从这些向量里extract 出主题，把相似的文档聚类在一起。

与语用推理的连接：在我的话语分析论文中，我用 BERTopic 识别求职叙事中的反复出现的话语模式。这些模式揭示了说话者如何隐含地传达字面内容之外的意义——比如用"挂了""上岸"等隐喻来建构身份、协商面子。这本质上是大规模的语用推理：计算方法帮助我们发现人们"言不由衷"的系统性模式。

Why linguistics rather than applied linguistics?（为什么选语言学而不是应用语言学）

My understanding is that general linguistics treats language itself as the object of study — its sound system, grammar, meaning, use, and cognitive basis. Applied linguistics is more concerned with how knowledge about language can address practical problems such as language teaching, acquisition, assessment, or language-related difficulties. The two fields overlap, especially in their use of corpora and experiments, but my current interest is closer to understanding how the language system itself works. For example, before evaluating an AI language product or an educational application, I think we first need to understand the linguistic system and the mechanisms of language use. Since I plan to pursue a PhD, general linguistics is a better fit for my current direction.
我的理解是，普通语言学把语言本身作为研究对象，研究它的语音系统、语法、意义、使用方式和认知基础；应用语言学则更关注如何利用语言知识解决语言教学、习得、测评或语言障碍等现实问题。两者会重叠，比如都可以使用语料库和实验方法，但我目前更关心语言系统本身如何运作。例如，在评价一个 AI 语言产品或教育应用之前，我认为首先要理解语言系统和语言使用机制。因为我计划读博，普通语言学更符合我目前的方向。

What was the most challenging part of your research?（研究中最困难的部分是什么）

Situation: During my iFlytek internship, I was asked to evaluate synthesised English speech across different varieties. Module 3: Projects

Corporate Image Construction (深讲主力)

30s version

Besides my graduation thesis, I have another paper in progress, developed from a course paper, using BERTopic and Appraisal Theory for critical discourse analysis, studying corporate image construction in job interview narratives on social media — focusing mainly on big-tech-related topics. The starting point was my observation that in big-tech recruitment contexts, vocabulary like "failed", "screwed", "made it ashore" is highly uniform, job-seekers widely position themselves as passive recipients, and comments don't question recruiters' evaluation criteria — only refine interview tips. A self-reinforcing evaluative discourse system. The big-tech recruitment context amplifies this discursive power structure, which is why I focus on big tech.
除了毕业论文，我还有一篇在写的论文，基于课程论文改进，用BERTopic 和评价理论做批评话语分析，研究社交媒体上——主要是大厂相关话题——的求职面试叙事中的企业形象建构。起因是我观察到大厂求职语境里，"挂了""凉了""上岸"这类词汇高度一致，求职者普遍把自己放在被动承受者位置，评论区也不质疑招聘方的评估标准，只完善面试技巧——形成了一套自我强化的评价话语体系。大厂的招聘语境把这种话语权力结构放大了，所以我聚焦大厂。

60s version (主力版本)

Methodologically, my corpus is approximately 22 million Chinese characters across two platforms — Zhihu's long-form answers and Weibo's short-form posts — spanning five years from 2021 to 2026. I use BERTopic for topic modelling to identify stable discourse topics, and Martin's Appraisal Theory for fine-grained qualitative analysis of these topics. I had originally drawn on Fairclough's three-dimensional framework, but my advisor suggested I background CDA rather than treat it as a theoretical framework — otherwise the theoretical layering becomes too crowded.
方法上，我的语料大约2200 万汉字，来自两个平台——知乎的长文回答和微博的短文帖子——覆盖 2021 到 2026 五年。我用 BERTopic 对语料做主题建模，识别出稳定的话语议题；用 Martin 的评价理论对这些议题做细致的质性分析。我原本还用了 Fairclough 的三维框架，但导师建议我把 CDA 背景化而不是作为理论框架——不然理论层确实太杂了。
The methodological contribution of this paper is: genuinely integrating computational methods with critical discourse analysis, not just layering them. Traditional CDA is often questioned for "representativeness" due to small samples. I use BERTopic to do topic modelling on large-scale corpus first, identifying stable discourse topics, then apply Appraisal Theory for fine-grained qualitative analysis on those topics. This preserves CDA's critical edge on power relations while gaining the scale support that computational methods provide.
这篇论文的方法论贡献在于：把计算方法和批评话语分析真正结合起来，而不是两张皮。传统 CDA 常因为样本小而受到"代表性"的质疑，而我用 BERTopic 先在大规模语料上做主题建模，识别出稳定的话语议题，再用评价理论对这些议题做细致的质性分析。这样既保留了 CDA 对权力关系的批判锋芒，又有了计算方法带来的规模支撑。

LFG 毕业论文 (45s)

My graduation thesis is a corpus-based study of intonation patterns of list-final items followed by "but" under the LFG (Lexical Functional Grammar) parallel architecture. For example, take a real sentence from my corpus: "Well, I count my iPod, cellphone and computer as technology but this has nothing to do with that." My thesis investigates what intonation pattern is used on "computer" — the list-final item before "but".
我的毕业论文是在LFG（词汇功能语法）平行架构下，用语料库方法研究list-final items followed by "but" 的语调模式。比如我语料库里真实的一句话："Well, I count my iPod, cellphone and computer as technology but this has nothing to do with that." 我论文研究的就是"computer" 这个 list-final item 用什么语调。
Methodologically, I used two corpora in a dual-design: Santa Barbara Corpus of Spoken American English (SBCSAE; Du Bois et al., 2000-2005, manually extracted and annotated) and Prosodically Annotated TED Talks (PANTED; Öktem et al., 2018, more items, providing statistical power). On my advisor's suggestion, I also used the ToBI annotation system to enhance academic rigor.
方法上，我用了双库设计：Santa Barbara 口语美式英语语料库（SBCSAE；Du Bois 等，2000-2005，人工提取和标注）和Prosodically Annotated TED Talks（PANTED；Öktem 等，2018，更多条目，提供统计效力）。在导师的建议下，我还使用了ToBI 标注系统来提升学术规范性。
This connects to a very timely methodological challenge — a recent paper by Dr. Zhihao Wang and Prof. Youngah Do at HKU (2026, Journal of East Asian Linguistics) addresses exactly the problem of identifying level tones in actual speech production. In my own research, I encountered this problem: I marked words that perceptually sounded like level tones, using them as markers of semantic incompleteness.
这连接到一个非常及时的方法论难题——港大Dr. Zhihao Wang 和 Prof. Youngah Do最近的一篇论文（2026，Journal of East Asian Linguistics）正好探讨了实际语音中识别平调的难题。我自己的研究也遇到了这个问题：我标出了听感上像平调的词，把它们作为语义未完结的标志。
This phonology work represents one methodological direction for me — qualitative analysis using established spoken corpora. I'm also exploring a computational approach in another in-progress paper on discourse analysis, where I crawled and built my own corpus from social media platforms, using BERTopic and Appraisal Theory to study...
这代表了我的一个方法论方向——使用现成口语语料库做定性分析。我也在另一篇在写论文中探索计算方法，自己从社交媒体爬取并构建语料，用 BERTopic 和评价理论做话语分析...

科大讯飞 (60s 大幅刷新版)

I work in iFlytek's Audio-Video Generation Resources Department doing speech data work in two main areas: translating English documents for the in-car voice assistant and establishing terminology consistency standards; quality-checking machine-synthesised English speech.
我在科大讯飞音视频生成资源部做语音数据相关工作，主要两块：给车载语音助手翻译英文文档、建立术语一致性规范；做机器合成英语语音的质检。
The QA side is most interesting. I systematically assess deviation between synthesised and natural speech at segmental and suprasegmental levels. The work later expanded to Singapore English and Australian English, forcing me outside the AmE/BrE standard framework.
质检这块最有意思。我需要从段级和超音段级系统评估合成语音和自然语音的偏差。后来工作范围扩到新加坡英语和澳洲英语，要求我跳出美英音的标准框架。
At the beginning, my mentor frequently challenged me — she thought I was finding too many errors, being too strict, no one had found this many before. 一开始我 mentor 经常质疑我——她觉得我找的错误太多、测评标准太严，之前从没人找出过这么多错。我就把理由说给她、找文献证明这种语音现象的合理性或不合理，查外国车企的表述、查 COCA 和 BNC 语料库。
The biggest by-product of this experience: I discovered a phonetic phenomenon that almost no existing literature has studied. Through discussions with my mentor and further research, I confirmed it and made it my graduation thesis topic — studying intonation patterns of list-final items followed by "but" under the LFG framework.
这段经历最大的副产品是：我发现了一个语音现象在现有文献里几乎没人研究过。在和 mentor 讨论和调研的过程中，我确认了这一点，于是把它作为了毕业论文选题——就是在 LFG 框架下研究 list-final items followed by "but" 的语调模式。
⚠️ 被追问才说 / Only say if asked:
How did you specifically evaluate? I assessed word pronunciation accuracy, intonation (sentence-medial and sentence-final), rhythm and pauses including speech rate, and errors caused by numbers, abbreviations, pinyin words, dates, and similar items.
具体怎么测评？我评估了单词发音准确性、语调（句中句末）、韵律停顿（包括语速），以及数字、缩略词、拼音词、日期等导致的读错。

吉林非遗大创 (60s 大幅改写版)

I initiated this project in my sophomore year, and it went through a strategic pivot mid-way. Perhaps a little earlier than I was ready for. As a result, I encountered obstacles at many stages.
这个项目是我大二发起的大创，过程中经历了战略转向。可能比我准备好要早了一些。因此我在很多阶段遇到了阻碍。
The original plan was a "Jilin ICH bilingual corpus" — collecting English introduction texts, doing discourse analysis to evaluate international communication effects. But during January-February 2025, I did a month of field visits to three major Jilin ICH museums and found the original plan infeasible: almost no foreign visitors during that period at any of the three museums; some had barely any English materials at all.
原计划做的是"吉林省非遗双语语料库"——收集英文介绍文本，做话语分析评估国际传播效果。但2025 年 1-2 月我对吉林省三座主要非遗博物馆做了一个月的实地走访，发现原计划不可行：三馆在此期间几乎没有外国游客到访，有的馆甚至基本没配备英文资料。
Based on this reality, I made a judgment: better to solve the more urgent "from nothing to something" problem than build a corpus nobody uses. So I pivoted the entire project from "corpus" to "termbase" construction — building a high-quality, time-limited open-source Chinese-English ICH termbase.
基于这个现实，我做了判断：与其做一个没人用的语料库，不如解决更紧迫的"从无到有"问题。于是把整个项目重心从"语料库"转向"术语库"建设——做一个高质量、限时开源的汉英非遗术语库。
I further wrote a reflective paper using Critical Heritage Studies' "Authorised Heritage Discourse" concept, questioning academia's optimistic discourse on ICH international communication feasibility — under conditions lacking audience base and communication infrastructure, broad international communication feasibility may be overestimated; recommended prioritising localised preservation.
我进一步写了一篇反思论文，用批判遗产研究的"权威化遗产话语"概念，质疑学界对非遗国际传播可行性的乐观论述——在缺乏受众基础与传播基础设施的条件下，广泛的国际传播可行性或被高估，建议回归在地保护优先。

CBL 法律翻译 (60s 大幅刷新版)

Six-month legal translation internship at CBL. I helped build a Chinese-English legal termbase and participated in the full translation workflow. This experience gave me two levels of training:
CBL 法律翻译实习做了半年，我协助建了中英法律术语库，参与完整翻译流程。这段经历给我两个层面的训练：
First, stylistic perception and control. Words like shall, hereby, pursuant to in legal texts made me realise style is a system.
第一是文体感知与控制。法律文本的 shall、hereby、pursuant to 这些词让我第一次意识到文体是一种系统。
Second, translation challenges in real contexts. Later I did chat-record translation — the parties were technical-domain speakers using jargon ("this is too hardcore"), also relying on context already built offline; I had difficulty understanding the Chinese itself. The company assigned me a translation mentor and a native editor; once I matured, their annotations shifted from "directly pointing out errors" to "Q&A format discussions" — what I learned wasn't just translation techniques but a cognitive habit: any text can be split and examined along two dimensions — "meaning accuracy" and "expression naturalness".
第二是真实语境的翻译挑战。后期我做了聊天记录翻译——双方是技术领域人士，说话带行话（"这个太硬核了"），还依赖线下已经构建好的语境，我理解中文本身就有困难。公司给我配了翻译 mentor 和母语者编辑，后期我成熟后，他们的批注从"直接指出错误"变成"问答形式和我商量"——我学到的不仅是翻译技巧，更是一种认知习惯：任何文本都可以从"意思准确"和"表达自然"两个维度拆开审视。
This experience directly spawned a corpus-based paper on the English translation of "non-full-time employment" in China's Labor Contract Law.
这段经历直接催生了一篇基于语料库的论文，研究《劳动合同法》中"非全日制用工"的英译。

AI 教育 Duolingo (45s)

This is an interdisciplinary project where I did two related sub-studies: one, sentiment analysis on L2 writing in the ICNALE corpus using VADER, TextBlob, and spaCy, trying to incorporate lexical richness and syntactic complexity into the model — I found a "false confidence" pattern among lower-proficiency learners, where the linguistic form is fluent and positive but the semantic content is empty; two, a Duolingo case study applying Bandura, Vygotsky, and educational equity theory to analyse how AI personalised learning impacts SES-related gaps, culminating in a five-dimensional "AI + educational equity" framework.
这是一个跨学科项目，我做了两个相互关联的子研究：一是用 VADER、TextBlob、spaCy 在 ICNALE 语料上做情感分析，尝试把词汇丰富度和句法复杂度纳入模型——发现低水平学习者里存在一种"虚假自信"模式，语言形式流畅积极，但语义内容是空的；二是用 Bandura、Vygotsky 和教育公平理论，对 Duolingo 做案例研究，分析 AI 个性化学习对 SES 差距的影响，最后提出了一个"AI + 教育公平"的五维框架。
This experience made me realise: AI educational products themselves should be treated as objects of linguistic research, not just as tools. That perspective shift affected me deeply and directly connects to the direction I want to pursue in my PhD — using discourse analysis frameworks to examine AI systems' linguistic output.
这段经历让我意识到：AI 教育产品本身应该被当作语言学研究对象，不只是工具。这种视角转换对我影响很大，也直接对接我博士想做的方向——用话语分析框架审视 AI 系统的语言输出。
